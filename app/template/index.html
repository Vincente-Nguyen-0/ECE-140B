Features

Engineered for the Outdoors

Every feature designed around one goal — reliable solar power wherever life takes you.

High-Efficiency Solar Panel

Monocrystalline solar cells built into the canopy deliver up to 100W of clean energy — even on partly cloudy days.

Multi-Device Charging

Four USB-A + USB-C ports with 65W PD fast charging. Simultaneously power phones, tablets, and laptops.

Theft Protection

Motion sensors and cable lock integration trigger instant push alerts to your phone if your station is tampered with.

GPS Tracking

Real-time location tracking built in. Know exactly where your umbrella is at all times via the E·Shady dashboard.

App-Connected

Monitor battery level, charging output, tilt angle, and weather conditions from your phone in real time.

Fully Portable

Weighs under 4 kg. Fold-flat design fits in a standard bag. Set up anywhere in under 60 seconds — beach, park, campsite.
